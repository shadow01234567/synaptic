Synaptic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Synaptic is an AI tool that helps small business owners create Google Ads Search campaigns. It is built by a team of four university students as a Final Year Project. Home page: https://synaptic-ads.streamlit.app. Contact: sashaheer.ali10@gmail.com.

What information we collect

  • Business information you type in: business name, website, description, city, budget and goals.
  • Google Ads account data (only if you connect an account): campaign, ad group, keyword and performance data of the account you authorize. We access it through the Google Ads API and OAuth, only with your permission.

How we use it

  • To research keywords and competitors, write ad copy, and build a campaign plan for your business.
  • If you click the push button, to create that campaign (paused by default) in the Google Ads account you authorized.
  • To show you reports about your campaigns.

We use Google user data only to provide these features to you. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not transfer it to others except as described below.

Who we share it with

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): the business description you type is sent to Anthropic's API to generate keywords, competitor research and ad text. Google Ads account data is not sent to Anthropic.
  • Google: campaign data you choose to push is sent to Google Ads.
  • Streamlit Community Cloud hosts the website.

We do not sell or rent your information. We may disclose it if the law requires.

Storage and retention

Results are generated for your session. We do not keep a long-term database of your Google Ads data. Authorization tokens are kept only as long as needed to run your request.

Your choices

You can revoke Synaptic's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask us to delete any information we hold, email us at the address above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Synaptic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
